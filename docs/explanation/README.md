# Explanation

Explanation pages give background and reasons. They are read away from the keyboard by someone who wants to understand why Guardline is built the way it is, and they make no attempt to be a procedure or a lookup table. For steps use the [How-To Guides](../how-to-guides/README.md), and for exact behavior the [Reference](../reference/README.md).

## Pages

- [Design](design.md): why every rule is data, why there are three entry points with one verdict, and what was left out on purpose.
- [Security Model](security-model.md): the threat model stated plainly, what Guardline protects against, what it does not, and the bypasses that are known and open.
- [Architecture](architecture.md): how a scan flows through the modules and why each piece sits where it does.
